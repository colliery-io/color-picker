URL scheme (for agents)
A quick hand-writable link auto-builds a board:
?bg=0e1116&p=7fb2ff,4bd07f,f06464&t=ff8787,3bc9db
• bg — background hex (no #)
• p — “Palette” section (grid)
• t — “Try” section (rows)
Copy share link emits a fuller ?d= token that preserves every section, title, layout, and color name.
Drag any card between sections · drag to reorder · hover a section for its controls.
